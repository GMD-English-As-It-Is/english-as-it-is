# How we started — a short log

A log of how Manny and Muse built this teaching project together,
from a first ESL class idea to a free, public learning site.

## The idea

Manny wanted to teach English — free, self-paced, learn by doing,
like freeCodeCamp for English. Classes would be hybrid, in person
and online, with fill-in-the-blanks exercises so students can take
part at their own level.

The project is named for his daughters, Galilea and Maria Jose —
Galimari Digital. Aprender • Crear • Crecer. Learn • Create • Grow.

## Log

### 2026-11-12 — The ESL class begins
Manny starts building an ESL class. Lessons 1–6 follow:
Introductions, Family & People, Daily Life, Food & shopping,
Places & directions, and Review & real conversation. A course pack,
a 10-minute first-day placement check, and a placement tracker
are added.

### 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-21 — The next level
Lessons 7–12 follow: Yesterday / the past, Last week / next week,
Months and Seasons, Describing people and places, Comparing people
and places, and Superlatives.

### 2026-11-21 — Body, Lesson 1, becomes a workbook
Manny sends a 6-page Body lesson. It becomes an interactive
workbook: 36 body words as picture flashcards with tap-to-listen
pronunciation, a space to write each word in the student's own
language, and 66 auto-graded questions.

Manny asks for a GitHub repository for the wordlists, organised as
"english-as-it-is" — Body as Lesson 1, Clothes as Lesson 2, and so
on. Free, self-paced, learn by doing.

### 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22 — The project goes public
The GitHub organisation GMD-English-As-It-Is is created, and the
repo GMD-English-As-It-Is/english-as-it-is is created. Manny sends
the whole 78-page Lane's ESL Book 1. All 5 wordlists are pushed:
Body, Clothes, Food, World, and School.

The GitHub Pages site goes live at
https://gmd-english-as-it-is.github.io/english-as-it-is/

The Clothes Lesson 2 workbook is built: 74 auto-graded questions.

### 2026-11-22 — Real audio, and two fixes
The workbooks first relied on the device's built-in speech, and
on many phones that meant silence. Muse replaces it with real
recorded MP3 audio: 113 clips for Lessons 1–2, one voice, slower
for learners.

Two bugs are found and fixed the same day: the Body workbook's
speaker was reading out the answer before the student chose, and
phone autofill was putting Spanish translations into the plural
answer boxes.

Donations are set up: a Sponsor button on GitHub, and a Ko-fi page
at https://ko-fi.com/gmdenglishasitis — free lessons, donations
welcome but never required.

### 2026-11-23 — Audio for Lessons 3–5
Manny asks for the same recorded audio for Food, World, and
School. 156 more MP3 clips are recorded and pushed. All 5 lessons
now have audio in the repo.

The README gains its dedication, in Manny's own words: "This
project is named for my daughters, Galilea and Maria Jose —
Galimari Digital. Aprender • Crear • Crecer."

### 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-25 — A paid side, on top of free
Manny asks for a paid members area on top of the free project.
The free lessons, audio, and workbooks stay free, always. The paid
side adds live teaching on top.

Membership tiers are drafted, approved, and pushed: Supporter
100 MXN/month, Conversation Club 200 MXN/month, 1-on-1 Learner
500 MXN/month. Prices are in Mexican pesos for learners in Mexico,
and the equivalent in the learner's currency outside Mexico,
because affordability is the aim.

### 2026-11-26 — Header, security, and the rules of the project
The header banner is added to the README and all 5 lesson pages.
The banner image lands as assets/banner.webp, after Manny signs
in as gmd-english-catering and turns on two-factor authentication.

The project gets its ethical foundation, committed straight to
main: a license (learning content CC BY 4.0, code MIT), full credit
to Dr. Richard R. Lane for Lane's ESL Book 1, and a Code of Conduct
— a kind, respectful place for non-native learners, where mistakes
are welcome.

## What this infrastructure is for

A free place where anyone, anywhere, can start learning English
at their own pace, with no account, no payment, and no fear of
making a mistake — and where a live teacher is an optional second
step, never a locked door.
