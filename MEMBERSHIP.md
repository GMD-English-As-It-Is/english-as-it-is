# Become a Member — English as it is

**Learn English free, at your own pace. Always.**

Everything already free stays free, forever: all 5 lessons, the recorded audio, and the interactive workbooks. Membership adds live teaching and extras on top — it never locks what already exists.

Prices are in Mexican pesos for learners in Mexico. Outside Mexico, pay the equivalent in your currency.

## Free — $0 MXN, forever

- All 5 lessons (Body, Clothes, Food, World, School) with wordlists and recorded audio
- Interactive workbooks (Body and Clothes now; Food, World, and School as they are built)
- Learn at your own pace, no sign-up, no cost

## Supporter — 100 MXN/month (about $5 USD)

For people who love the project and want to keep it free for everyone.

- Everything in Free
- Early access to new lessons and workbooks before they go public
- Downloadable PDF packs of the lessons
- Your name on the Supporters list in this repo (only if you want it)

## Conversation Club — 200 MXN/month (about $10 USD)

For learners who want to actually speak.

- Everything in Supporter
- One live group conversation class per week (online, 60 minutes)
- Practice prompts and vocabulary for each week's class
- Member-only posts with extra exercises

## 1-on-1 Learner — 500 MXN/month (about $25 USD)

For learners who want a teacher of their own.

- Everything in Conversation Club
- One private 1-on-1 lesson per month (60 minutes — individual, conversation, or speech-only)
- Personal feedback on pronunciation and homework
- Priority booking for extra private lessons

## Private lessons (no membership needed)

- Private lesson, 1-on-1: 150–300 MXN per hour (about $8–$15 USD)
- Group class: 100 MXN per student per hour (about $5 USD)
- Members get 20% off extra private lessons
