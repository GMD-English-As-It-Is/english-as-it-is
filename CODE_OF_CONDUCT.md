# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

English as it is is a place to learn English, for everyone — especially
for learners who are not native speakers. Mistakes are part of learning
here. We welcome people of every background, level, language, age,
and ability.

We promise to be kind, patient, and respectful in all of our
interactions in this project.

## How we treat each other

- Be kind and respectful. Listen first, and assume good intent.
- Be patient with learners. Never mock a mistake, an accent, or a
  simple question. A simple question is a good question.
- Use welcoming language. No harassment, no insults, no hateful
  language, and no unwanted sexual attention.
- Respect different views and experiences. Disagree politely, and
  focus on the idea, not the person.
- Help others learn. If you correct someone, do it gently and
  explain why.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, bullying, or threats of any kind.
- Hateful, insulting, or discriminatory language.
- Spam, scams, or sharing someone's private information.
- Pretending to be someone else, or taking credit for someone
  else's work.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct,
please tell us. You can open an issue in this repository, or email
gmd.englishasitis@gmail.com.

The maintainer will review the report, listen to everyone involved,
and decide what to do. That may include a warning, removing a
comment or contribution, or, in serious cases, asking someone to
leave the project.

## Our responsibility

This Code of Conduct applies everywhere in this project — in the
repository, in issues and discussions, and in the live classes and
community spaces linked from it.

We are here to learn together. Aprender • Crear • Crecer.
