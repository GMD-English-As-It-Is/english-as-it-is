## of

of shows the existence of an organic or unbreakable connection between things.

- This is a map of Great Britain.
- This is a map of Asia.
- That is a skin of an orange.
- That is a part of my watch.

## the

If "this is one example of a thing," put a/an: a map, a watch, etc. If a thing is special or particular, different from all others of its kind, put the before a noun: This is the map of America. Do not put the before nouns with general meanings: Coffee is brown. Sugar is sweet. Lemons are yellow.

- Water is cold, but the water of my tea is very hot.
- Bread is white, but the bread of Russia is black.
- Bananas are yellow, but the bananas on his shoulder are not yellow. They are green.
- These bracelets are not expensive, but the bracelet on her left arm is very expensive.
- Some candy is on the bottom of his shoe.
- Some nuts are on top of the cake.
- The legs of his pants are too short.
- The right arm of her coat is wet, but the other arm is dry.

## in / out of

in is normally inside something.

- Some money is in my coat pocket.
- Some water is in her ear.
- Much juice is in this orange.
- Is she in England or Italy today?
- Only six eggs are in this big, beautiful, brown sugar cake.
- He is in the water, not on it!
- The meat is fried in only butter and lemon juice.
- Many little parts are in this old clock.
- Is the chicken in or out of the egg? — It is out of the egg.
- Is the fish in or out of the water? — It is out of the water.

## Past — to be

- Was it on your skin? — Was it not on your skin?

## close to / far from / also

- Is New York far from Los Angeles? — Yes, New York is far from Los Angeles.
- Is New York close to Washington, D.C.? — Yes, New York is close to Washington, D.C.
- Is Beijing close to Shanghai? — Yes, Beijing is close to Shanghai.
- Are the Philippine Islands far from Hawaii? — Yes, the Philippine Islands are very far from Hawaii.
- Is Tokyo close to or far from Osaka? — Tokyo is not too far from Osaka.
- Is Florida close to or far from Cuba? — Florida is very close to Cuba.
- Her clothes are beautiful and also expensive.
- Her hair is long and also brown.
- Cuba is an island and Jamaica is also.
- The United States of America is a big nation and Canada is also.
- This is a map of not only West Europe, but also East Europe.
- These apples are not only fresh, but also sweet.

## Where...? / Many... / Much...

- What is Tokyo? — It is a big city. Where is it? — Tokyo is in Japan.
- What is Japan? — It is an island nation. Where is it? — Japan is in Asia.
- What is Italy? — It is a nation. Where is it? — Italy is south from Switzerland.
- What is Sicily? — It is an island that is a part of Italy. Where is it? — Sicily is close to the toe of the Italian boot.

- Many fruits are sweet. — Much juice is sweet.
- Many rings are expensive. — Much food is expensive.
- Many fish are in the ocean. — Much salt is in the ocean.
- Many nations are in Asia. — Much tea is from Asia.
- Many buttons are on her dress. — Much pepper is in this soup.
- Many islands are on this map. — Much oil is in the nations of Central Africa.
- Many parts of the world are hot and dry. — Much sugar is from South America.
- Many cities are between here and there. — Much distance is between here and there.
- Many of my friends were here. — Much of the time he was in New York.
- Too many of the apples were sour. — Too much of the milk was sour.

## What time is it?

- What time is it? — It is 6 o'clock.
- What time is it? — It is 7 o'clock.
- What time is it? — It is 8:10.
- What time is it in San Francisco? — It is 9:23 a.m.
- What time is it in New York? — It is 12:23 p.m. in New York.
- What time is it in Mexico City? — It is 10:15 a.m.
- What time is it in Caracas? — It is 1:15 p.m. in Caracas.
